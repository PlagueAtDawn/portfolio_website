Subject four - Premere Pro VS Clipchamp

When you use Premere Pro, you get to use every tool possible under the sun for editing. You can edit small vedios and high quality videos. It's the standard in the professional world, since you can add many effects and scale as much as you want. However it's also harder to learn overall since there's that much you can do with it, it takes a lot of time to get into everything and it's not always you will use everything it can. With Clipchamp you have a easier time using it since theres less to learn and it's also come with Widnows 11 and it's a free to use program where Premere cost 200kr a month with a free trail for a week only.

The overall Pros and Cons for Premere Pro is; Very good for editing bigger videos, very good for professional work, Really good overlays and captions. Longer setup time, Not easy of use, harder interface behavor

The overall Pros and Cons for Clipchamp is; Already installed from the start on Windows 11, free, fast setup time, Good interface behavor. Less editing compatibility, missing features for professional use, not as good with overlays and captions.
